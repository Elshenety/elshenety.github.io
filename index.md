## About Me
I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate at Bilkent University, specializing in the design, microfabrication, and multiphysics modeling of advanced capacitive micromachined ultrasonic transducers.While conventional CMUTs typically require relatively high operating voltages, my research focuses on **Capacitive Micromachined Ultrasonic Transducers with a Force Plate (CMUT-FP) architecture**. The goal is to develop low-voltage, high-sensitivity, and wide-band CMUT-based transducers for wearable ultrasound applications.
## Research & Technical Portfolio

### 1. CMUT Design & Microfabrication
*   **Architectures:** Specialized in CMUTs to optimize sensitivity, bandwidth, and average displacement.
*   **Cleanroom Fabrication:** Hands-on experience in developing complete microfabrication process flows. 
*   **Stiction-Free Release:** Experienced in implementing **dry release microfabrication plans** using Xenon Difluoride ($XeF_2$) etching to mitigate stiction and improve device yield compared to traditional wet etching methods.
*   **Mentorship:** Served as a Teaching Assistant for the Microfabrication Course at Bilkent University, training students in cleanroom protocols and micro-device processing.

### 2. Multiphysics Modeling & Simulation
*   **Finite Element Analysis (FEA):** Extensive experience in high-performance acoustic and structural simulations using **OnScale** and **ANSYS**.
*   **Analytical Modeling:** Proficient in lumped element circuit modeling and static/dynamic behavior optimization of micromachined membranes.

### 3. Acoustic Characterization & Experimental Setup
*   Characterization of fabricated ultrasonic transducers, including electrical impedance measurements, surface profiling, and acoustic pressure field mapping.
